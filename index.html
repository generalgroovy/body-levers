What this model means

An idealized lever turns around a fixed hinge. Gravity pulls the load down. The green arrow pulls at a right angle to the lever to balance that turning effect.

This mechanical learning model omits the lever’s own weight, friction, motion, and biological anatomy. “Model pull” is a calculated force, not your muscle effort, a lifting recommendation, or a measure of safety. The illustration is a mechanism rather than a human body diagram.
